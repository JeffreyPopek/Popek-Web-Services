About the founder

Hello, I'm Jeffrey Popek.

Founder & Web Developer

I'm a San Francisco-based programmer with a bachelor's degree in Game Programming from Champlain College. I've also worked with local churches to build and improve their websites.

As a churchgoer myself, I'm familiar with how churches operate, including how vestries make decisions and how things can sometimes take time to get approved. I understand that process, and I want to make building or updating a website as easy as possible for everyone involved.

At Popek Web Services, my goal is to create simple, reliable websites that are easy to use and maintain. You'll be working directly with me throughout the process, so you'll always know who to reach out to if you have questions or need something changed.
